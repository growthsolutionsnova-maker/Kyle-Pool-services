FAQ

Questions pool owners ask us.

Don't see your question? Call or text — we're happy to help.

(561) 305-9345
How much does pool service cost?

Every pool is different — size, equipment, screen enclosure, trees and how often it's used all change the work involved. That's why we don't publish flat prices. Call or text (561) 305-9345 and we'll give you a free, no-pressure estimate for your pool.

What's included in weekly pool service?

Each visit includes skimming the surface, brushing walls, steps and tile, vacuuming as needed, emptying skimmer and pump baskets, testing and balancing your water chemistry, and a quick check of your pump and filter.

Do I need to be home for service?

No. As long as we have access to the pool area — a gate code, key or unlocked gate — we'll take care of everything and let you know when your pool is done.

Which cities do you serve?

We serve Boca Raton, Deerfield Beach, Delray Beach, Boynton Beach, Lake Worth, Lighthouse Point, Coconut Creek and Parkland across Palm Beach and Broward County.

How often should a pool be serviced in South Florida?

Weekly. Florida heat, strong sun, summer rain and falling leaves burn through chlorine and feed algae fast. A weekly visit keeps your water balanced and prevents costly problems.

Can you fix a green pool?

Yes. Our Green-to-Clean service treats the algae, cleans the pool and filter, and rebalances the water until it's clear again. Call us and we'll tell you what your pool needs.

Do you repair pumps, filters and heaters?

Yes. We diagnose and repair pool pumps, motors, filters and heaters, and we'll always explain the problem and your options before doing any work.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Coughlin Pool Service is fully licensed and insured, so your home and your pool are protected.

What are your hours?

We work Monday through Saturday, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Call or text (561) 305-9345 anytime during those hours.

How do I get started?

Call or text (561) 305-9345, or send the Free Estimate form on this page. We'll ask a few questions about your pool, schedule a quick visit if needed, and get your service started.
